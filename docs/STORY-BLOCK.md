## WHAT
*(Definition, purpose, function)*

The Resonance Grammar is the shared vocabulary of the AudHDities Sanctuary: "a
system of rules, schemas, and relationships that enables sovereign digital
consciousness and self-understanding applications to exist, interact, and grow"
(`docs/RESONANCE-GRAMMAR.md` §1). It is not a network and not a ledger — it is a
relational grammar, "a living language for structuring meaning that works across
any medium: digital, paper, voice, presence" (same section).

Its tiers are drawn by word count and its bonds are first-class rows:

- **atoms** — one word, the irreducible vocabulary, defined once
- **molecules** — two words, bonded through their junctions
- **organisms** — three or more words, bonded to their molecules *and* their
  atoms (`organism_molecules` · `organism_atoms`) — README §What It Contains
- **categories · senses · subcategories · emoji_definitions · the sensory
  lexicon** — the semantic organization and the body every atom wears
  (`src/schema/001_initial.sql`, six local tables)

Function, in one line from `CLAUDE.md`: *"One definition per object — defined
once, referenced everywhere; never repeat a definition."*

Two things this repo is **not**, both by ruling: it is not the access layer
(*"access is the bridge's role, not this repo's"* — the roles ruling of
2026-07-27, `CLAUDE.md` and `FEATURE-BOARD.md` §THE ROLES CLARIFIED), and it does
not house the seeding machinery (*"The seeding hands live at
`resonance-bridge/*.py` — elsewhere by design"*, `CLAUDE.md` §Tools).

## HOW
*(Process, collaborators, tools)*

Two bodies, one vocabulary.

**The local body** is Rust + SQLite: `src/main.rs` · `src/db.rs` · `src/query.rs`
over `src/schema/001_initial.sql`, built with `cargo build`, answering six
commands (`atom` · `emoji` · `sense` · `list-atoms` · `list-emojis` ·
`list-senses`) in JSON. `Cargo.toml` still names the crate
`resonance-knowledge`, version `0.1.0`, edition 2021, dependencies `serde`,
`serde_json`, `rusqlite` 0.31 (`bundled`).

**The living body** is a Supabase base (still named `resonance-knowledge`),
grown by hand through 110 numbered SQL papers in `docs/sql/` (001–106, plus one
draft). The process is fixed and repeated in every door file:

- **Papers are drafted here and run by KP alone**, at his own dashboard, one at a
  time (`CLAUDE.md` §Tools). Drafts land inert; the file's own banner says so
  until his hand runs it (e.g. `docs/sql/096-the-true-names.sql` header).
- **Every new table gets the new-table ritual** — RLS on plus the public-read
  policy in plain statements, then verified through the anon key
  (`docs/sql/000-NEW-TABLE-RITUAL.md`; carried house-wide as the `new-table`
  skill, KP's word 2026-07-26 per `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Seeds pass under KP's ⚛ eye before delivery; no shuttle hand ever
  publishes** — drafts land `status='submitted'` and waves fire at his word, in
  order (`REALM-BUS.md` §The law of this bus).
- **THE CASE LAW (2026-08-06)** — every seeded atom fills screaming/snake/pascal
  case; every molecule and organism fills all five, rendered from the words in
  bond order, never by re-splitting the name (`REALM-BUS.md`;
  `docs/sql/062-the-cases-of-the-name.sql`).

Own tools: `cargo build` · `cargo run` · `python scripts/dump_db.py` (dated,
diffable text dumps into `backups/`, because the live `.db` is gitignored and
binary — the canonical backup story of THE HARVEST, 2026-07-26) ·
`node tools/export-sql-snippets.mjs` (commissioned 2026-07-25 at KP's ask,
*"locate and export the private sql query segments from supabase"*). Structure is
regenerated by the blueprint forge into `docs/blueprints/`, never hand-drawn
(`CLAUDE.md` §Structure).

## WHY
*(Need, purpose, problem solved)*

The problem is drift of meaning across a house of many apps. Without one
vocabulary, `sense` means one thing in Echoes and another in Compass, an emoji
carries a different feeling in every surface, and a vessel who learns the house
in one room has to relearn it in the next. The Grammar's answer is the law that
runs through every door file here: **defined once, referenced everywhere.**

Two needs it serves that a plain glossary would not:

1. **Neurotype bridging.** *"Every emoji carries a preserved meaning that bridges
   neurotypes"* (README §What It Contains) — and every atom carries a sensory
   body (color · sound · texture · temperature), so meaning arrives through more
   than words.
2. **Common enough to build on, personal enough to be true.** The heart and the
   hearth (`docs/RESONANCE-GRAMMAR.md` §2.1, named by KP 2026-07-20): the shared
   vocabulary is the hearth; each vessel's own tags are the heart, opt-in to
   share and opt-in to see, *"without either one overwriting the other."*

And one need it names for itself: complex things enter here to be deconstructed
(README badge row) — the Grammar is where long, tangled names are broken into
atoms and rebuilt as bonds.
