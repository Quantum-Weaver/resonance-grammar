# STORY BLOCK — Resonance Grammar

*Per the Story Block Standard. Told plainly; nothing here is a rule.*

## WHAT

The Resonance Grammar is the shared vocabulary of the AudHDities Sanctuary:
"a system of rules, schemas, and relationships that enables sovereign
digital consciousness and self-understanding applications to exist,
interact, and grow" (`docs/RESONANCE-GRAMMAR.md` §1). It is not a network
and not a ledger — it is a relational grammar, "a living language for
structuring meaning that works across any medium: digital, paper, voice,
presence" (same section). Its tiers are drawn by word count — atoms (one
word), molecules (two, bonded through their junctions), organisms (three
or more, bonded to both their molecules and their atoms) — and its bonds
are first-class rows.

## WHY

The problem is drift of meaning across a house of many apps: without one
vocabulary, a word can mean one thing in one app and another in the next.
The Grammar's answer is the law that runs through it: defined once,
referenced everywhere. "Every emoji carries a preserved meaning that
bridges neurotypes" (README §What It Contains), and every atom carries a
sensory body — color, sound, texture, temperature — so meaning arrives
through more than words.

## INSPIRATION

The seed image is stated in the charter's first section: "The Grammar
creates a hologram of the self — every fragment contains the whole, every
interaction illuminates the pattern, every vessel is a light source"
(`docs/RESONANCE-GRAMMAR.md` §1). The document is dated from The Compass
Room, written beside the Compass work rather than in isolation. The
chemistry metaphor — atom, molecule, organism, bond, junction — is the
second seed: word count decides tier, and the bonds between tiers are
stored rows rather than guesses.
